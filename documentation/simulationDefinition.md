# Simulation Definition #

This document provides a detailed definition of the simulation, including its objectives, parameters, and expected outcomes. The simulation is designed to model the behavior of agents as they navigate and climb a surface, taking into account various environmental factors and agent characteristics.

## Objectives : ##

The primary objectives of the simulation are to:
1. Model the behavior of agents in a dynamic environment.
2. Analyze the impact of environmental factors on agent behavior.
3. Provide a visual representation of agent interactions and climbing behavior. 

## Parameters : ##

The simulation includes several adjustable parameters that influence agent behavior and environmental conditions. These parameters include:
- **Agent Speed**: The speed at which agents move across the surface.
- **Climbing Ability**: The ability of agents to climb different surfaces, which may vary based on their characteristics.
- **Surface Roughness**: The texture of the surface, which can affect the ease with which agents can climb.
- **Environmental Obstacles**: The presence of obstacles in the environment that agents must navigate around or over.
- **Agent Population**: The number of agents present in the simulation, which can affect interactions and competition for resources.    

## Expected Outcomes : ##

The expected outcomes of the simulation include:
1. A better understanding of how agents interact with their environment and each other.
2. Insights into the factors that influence climbing behavior and success rates.
